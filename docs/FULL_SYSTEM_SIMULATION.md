# Full System Simulation

## Objective

Validate the complete Zecpath AI recruitment pipeline by simulating the end-to-end hiring workflow and verifying the accuracy, consistency, and reliability of AI decisions.

---

## Workflow

Resume Upload
↓
Resume Parsing
↓
ATS Scoring
↓
Screening Evaluation
↓
HR Interview
↓
Technical Interview
↓
Cross-Round Aggregation
↓
Final Recommendation
↓
Hiring Report Generation

---

## Simulation Stages

### 1. Resume Upload
- Candidate uploads resume.
- Resume is parsed and cleaned.

### 2. ATS Scoring
- Skills, education, and experience are evaluated.
- ATS score is generated.

### 3. Screening
- Candidate eligibility is checked.
- Screening score is generated.

### 4. HR Interview
- HR questions are generated.
- Communication, confidence, and behavioral skills are evaluated.

### 5. Technical Interview
- Technical responses are evaluated.
- Technical score is generated.

### 6. Final Recommendation
- Scores from all rounds are aggregated.
- AI generates the hiring recommendation.

---

## AI vs Human Comparison

- Compare AI recommendation with human recruiter decision.
- Detect agreement or disagreement.

---

## Consistency Validation

- Compare ATS Score
- HR Score
- Technical Score

Identify inconsistencies across evaluation rounds.
